Who We Are

Wine Tastings for Real People (No Pretension)

We started monthly wine tastings in Colchester because we got tired of feeling like you needed a degree to enjoy a decent bottle. So we created something better.

🗺️

We Explore the World

Every month we pick bottles from different regions — from familiar French classics to exciting discoveries from Georgia, South Africa, and beyond.

📖

We Speak Plain English

Our tasting notes are written by humans for humans. "Dark fruit and a smooth finish" — not "notes of sun-baked Mediterranean schist." You're welcome.

🎉

We Make Nights Out of It

Monthly tastings, themed evenings, and socials around Colchester. Come solo, bring a friend, or drag your partner along. Everyone fits in here.
